DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications

Free, open-source Control4 driver: notifications as a pop-up on LG webOS TVs. Part of DirectorLink Drivers.

https://directorlink.io/drivers/lg-tv

A doorbell ring, a camera alert, a door or gate opened, the alarm: on the TV, over whatever is on. On the home network only: no cloud, no LG account, no ThinQ.

Setup

One driver per TV, in the TV's room.

  1. Give the TV a fixed IP address in the router, and turn the TV on (not standby).
  2. Pick the TV in TVs Found (the driver looks for LG TVs when it is added; Find TVs looks again), or type its address in TV IP Address.
  3. Accept on the TV with its remote: within a few seconds the TV asks whether to allow a new device. The words are the TV's own and may not name DirectorLink. Status says Paired.
  4. Run Test Notification: the TV shows Control4: Test: pop-ups from Control4 work on this TV.
  5. Choose what this TV shows (Doorbell, Camera Alerts, Doors and Gates, Alarm, Other) and Quiet Hours.

The TV asks once. If nobody accepts (the TV asks for about two minutes), or it is refused, the driver does not ask again by itself: run Pair Again with the TV on.

When a pop-up shows

Only when the TV is on. Off, in standby or with its screen off, the pop-up is dropped with one line in the log, and Notification Failed fires. The driver never turns the TV on. The TV has 3 seconds to answer; when the driver is not connected at that moment, it connects at once.

One pop-up every 5 seconds at most on each TV; the others wait their turn, a doorbell or the alarm first. Only the kinds switched on, nothing during Quiet Hours (Show Notification ignores the kinds; Test Notification always shows). The text is shown as sent, as Title: Message, Hebrew included. No picture: many webOS versions show nothing at all when a pop-up carries one.

The connection: port 3001 (TLS; the TV's own certificate, the same on every LG TV, is taken as it is), port 3000 on TVs before 2018. A ping every 15 seconds; when the TV goes away, the driver tries again after 5 s, 30 s, 2 min, then every 5 min. The TV's key is never logged or shown; it can only show pop-ups and read whether the TV is on.

OffThe TV is off or in standby (it answered at this address before).
Not reachableNo TV has answered at this address yet: check the address and that the TV is on.

Programming reference

Command: SHOW_NOTIFICATION (Title, Message): a pop-up, whatever the kinds, not during Quiet Hours. Events: Notification Shown · Notification Failed (off, not reachable, no answer within 3 seconds, or not paired). Variable: LAST_NOTIFICATION (STRING), the text of the last pop-up shown or failed, set before the event fires. For several TVs, add Show Notification for each TV in the same When.

DirectorLink

DirectorLink 1.13 and newer sends its notifications to this driver as soon as both are in the project (DirectorLink is not required). The driver follows the DirectorLink notification agreement, version 1:

WhatThe driver
MarkerString variable DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY = 1, set at every start, after the driver's other variables (never reordering them).
KindsString variable DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS: the kinds switched on, comma-separated, from doorbell, camera, door, alarm, other; updated whenever a property changes.
A notificationThe command DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY by C4:SendToDevice, taken in ExecuteCommand and ReceivedFromProxy, with VERSION (1), ID (unique; a repeat within 10 minutes is ignored), KIND, TITLE and MESSAGE (final text in the home's language, shown exactly as given), PICTURE (optional, an http address on the controller with a JPEG for 60 seconds; not used by this driver).
RulesNever block. Only the kinds switched on, nothing during Quiet Hours. At most one pop-up every 5 seconds per TV, a doorbell or the alarm first. Set LAST_NOTIFICATION, then fire Notification Shown or Notification Failed.

The variables come in this order: LAST_NOTIFICATION, DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY, DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS.
