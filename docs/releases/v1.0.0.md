## DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications 1.0.0

First full release. Add the driver for an LG TV, accept it once with the TV's remote, and DirectorLink's notifications (a doorbell ring, a camera alert, a door or gate opened, the alarm) pop up on the TV. Composer programming can show its own.

Free, open-source Control4 driver for LG webOS TVs. Part of [DirectorLink Drivers](https://directorlink.io/drivers). Free. No subscription, no license key, no account with us. Works on its own: DirectorLink is not required.

### Tested on

A real LG TV in an installer's project: pairing with the TV's remote and pop-ups from Control4 confirmed. The driver is the same as 1.0.0-beta.1. Reports from other models and webOS versions are welcome.

### What it does

- **On the home network only**: the TV's own local API (a WebSocket on port 3001, or 3000 on TVs before 2018). No cloud, no LG account, no ThinQ.
- **Pairs once**: the TV asks, you accept with the remote. The TV's key is kept in the project and never logged or shown. The driver asks the TV only for pop-ups and its power state. Works with LG's 2026 firmware (webOS 26).
- **Only when the TV is on**: off, in standby, or no answer within 3 seconds, the pop-up is dropped with one log line. It never turns the TV on.
- **Your choice per TV**: Doorbell, Camera Alerts, Doors and Gates, Alarm, Other (Doorbell and Alarm on), and Quiet Hours.
- **One pop-up every 5 seconds at most**, a doorbell or the alarm first.
- **Text as sent**, Hebrew and other right-to-left languages included.
- **Find TVs**: picks the TV from the ones on the network, or type its IP address.
- **Programming**: Show Notification (Title, Message); Notification Shown, Notification Failed; `LAST_NOTIFICATION`.
- **With DirectorLink 1.13**: the DirectorLink notification agreement, version 1 (`DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY`, `DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY_KINDS`, the command `DIRECTORLINK_NOTIFY`), documented in the README.

### Installing

1. Upload the file with **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent**.
2. Give the TV a fixed IP address and turn it on.
3. Search **DirectorLink**, add **DirectorLink · LG TV Notifications** to the TV's room, pick the TV in **TVs Found** (or type its IP address), and accept on the TV with its remote.
4. Run **Test Notification**.

Requirements: Control4 OS 3.3.0 or newer, Composer Pro, an LG TV with webOS. Full steps: [directorlink.io/drivers/lg-tv](https://directorlink.io/drivers/lg-tv).

### Updating

- **From 1.0.0-beta.1:** **Driver → Add or Update Driver or Agent** with this file. Settings, the TV's key and programming stay; the TV does not ask again.

### Known issues

- **The words on the TV's question are the TV's own**: they may not name DirectorLink.
- **No picture in the pop-up**: many webOS versions show nothing at all when a pop-up carries one. A notification's picture is not used.
- **Find TVs** finds only TVs that are on: type the IP address when it finds nothing.
- **A TV that keeps its network on in standby** counts as off by its own power state; an old webOS that does not report it (webOS 2, for one) counts as on while connected.
